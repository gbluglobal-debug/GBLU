About GBLU

What is GBLU?

Global Business & Leadership Union (GBLU) is a growing community built around four pillars: Leadership, Business, Entrepreneurship and Connection.

GBLU aims to bring ambitious people together to learn from one another, develop practical skills, exchange ideas, discover opportunities and build meaningful relationships.

The community begins with students and young people, but its vision goes beyond the student community to include young professionals, entrepreneurs, business leaders, mentors, professionals, organizations and companies.

Why GBLU

Ambition needs a community

Ambitious people often have ideas and motivation, but may lack connections, guidance, practical experience, exposure, mentorship, opportunities or a professional network. GBLU aims to help close that gap.

Our Four Pillars

Four areas at the heart of the GBLU community.

Leadership

Develop people who can lead with confidence, responsibility and purpose.

GBLU aims to create opportunities for people to develop leadership skills, communication, teamwork, decision-making and responsibility.

02

Understand business, exchange ideas and learn from the real world.

Entrepreneurship

GBLU aims to encourage entrepreneurial thinking, innovation, problem-solving, startup discussions and learning from entrepreneurs.

04
